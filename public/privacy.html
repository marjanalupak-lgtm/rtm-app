RTM tree — Privacy policy

RTM tree is a browser app for keeping a Requirements Traceability Matrix.

Where your data lives

Every project is a Google Sheet created in your own Google Drive (folder “RTM”). The app runs entirely in your browser and talks directly to Google's APIs. There is no RTM tree server, database or analytics; the app's developer cannot see your projects.

What access the app asks for

  • drive.file — access only to files the app creates or that you explicitly open with it (via the Google file picker). The app cannot see the rest of your Drive.
  • email / profile — to show who you are and to record who made each change in the project history.

Your sign-in token is kept only in your browser tab for up to one hour.

Sharing

Only you and the people you share a project with (via Google Drive sharing) can see it. You can remove access or delete the files in Google Drive at any time, and revoke the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
